Summary

ClipCast is a private desktop tool that uploads [YOUR NAME / CHANNEL]'s own videos to its own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It runs on the owner's computer. It has no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not sell or share data.

What data ClipCast accesses

  • Login tokens issued by Google and TikTok when you connect an account (OAuth). These let ClipCast act on your behalf.
  • Basic account info: your YouTube channel name and ID, and your TikTok display name, avatar and open ID (user.info.basic), shown so you can see which account is connected.
  • Your video files and the text you provide (titles, descriptions, captions, privacy choices), read from your computer only when you ask to upload them.
  • Upload results, such as video IDs and links, kept in a local history.

How the data is used

Only to upload, schedule and check the status of videos you choose, and to show you which accounts are connected. TikTok scopes used: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish. Google scope used: YouTube upload and read access for your own channel.

Where it is stored

All tokens and upload history are stored only on your own computer, in %APPDATA%\ClipCast. Videos and text are sent directly from your computer to Google or TikTok over encrypted HTTPS connections. Nothing is sent to the operator or any other third party.

Sharing

ClipCast does not sell, rent or share your data. Data goes only to YouTube or TikTok, as needed to complete the uploads you request.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnect an account in ClipCast to delete its saved login.
  • Delete %APPDATA%\ClipCast\tokens.json to remove all saved logins.
